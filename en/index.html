Statement

I am a customer success manager at Lenovo Baiying, responsible for private-domain operations and channel new media under the channel and franchise division. One side: helping three hundred-plus IT service stores retain customers and build repeatable in-store mechanics. The other: running 102 short-video accounts to pull franchisees in. They look like two jobs, but they are the same job — turning attention into arrivals, and arrivals into retention.

I am not only a person who reads data, nor only a person who writes content. Eight of nine KPIs landed in H1 FY26/27, with new customers at 129% of time progress. That is the number I am proudest of — not because it looks good, but because behind it sits a process someone else can run: standards, scripts, diagnostics and reviews, all written down.

The three things outside work are the same habit: build first, then turn it into a method. Eight posters for Napi Coffee produced one iron rule — AI for the image, code for the type. A product script for the outdoor account took four drafts to land on its final line. AI went into scripts, storyboards, leads, edits and reviews, and then the experience became an SOP someone else can inherit. I work the way people learn by doing: start, think while building, document afterwards.
