Here AI is not an assistant. It participates in production. Bulk script generation; AI video prompts broken into four movements (0–3s hook, 3–7s sensory detail, 7–11s emotional release, 11–15s resonant close); comment scraping on Douyin feeding AI intent scoring; batch re-edits across 102 accounts; AI-assisted review inside the weekly meeting.

The most valuable one is intent tiering: scrape → score → grade → prioritise, replacing manual first-pass screening and cutting lead-handling time by two thirds. Tier A asked how to join; tier C was just watching. One level apart, entirely different follow-up order.

The honest weakness: this still lives in my head. It is not yet a documented, transferable standard. If I leave, the knowledge leaves with me. So the goal for the next half is precise — turn a personal tool into a team asset.
